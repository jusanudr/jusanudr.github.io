Cuddle Baskets – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Cuddle Baskets is a puzzle game made by Jusanudr. It is made for everyone, including children. This page explains what information the game uses. In short: we do not collect any personal information, there are no accounts, and the game does not track you.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for your name, email address, photos, contacts or location, and we (the makers of the game) do not receive any information about you.

Information kept on your device

The game saves your level, star count, sound settings and which lessons you have seen. This is stored only on your own device so you can continue where you left off. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Ads

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Because the game is made for children too, every ad request is set up to be child-safe:

  • Ads are requested as child-directed (following the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA, and similar laws).
  • Only general-audience (G-rated) ads are allowed.
  • Ads are not personalized: they are not based on your interests or on what you do in other apps.
  • The game does not use the device's advertising ID.

To show an ad and to prevent fraud, Google's ad service may automatically receive limited technical information from the device, such as the IP address, device type and app version. Google handles this information under its own privacy policy, and not for personalized advertising: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Some ads are optional reward videos that players can choose to watch for an extra undo, restart or reveal. The game always asks first.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The game has no chat, no sharing, no in-app purchases and no links to social media.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
